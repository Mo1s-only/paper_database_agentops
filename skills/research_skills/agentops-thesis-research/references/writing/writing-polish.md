# Writing Polish Workflow

Use this only after the thesis section's claims, evidence, citations, and terminology are stable.

This workflow combines the user-recommended Humanizer skill with Research Paper Writing Skills. It should make prose clearer and more natural without changing the scientific meaning.

## Preconditions

Do not run a Humanizer-style polish while any of these remain unresolved:

- unsupported central claim;
- missing citation for a literature claim;
- unstable terminology;
- unclear experiment result;
- unresolved contradiction;
- unknown thesis requirement.

If any precondition fails, return to `thesis-writing.md` or `../quality/quality-gates.md`.

## Humanizer-Style Pass

Edit for natural academic prose while preserving content.

Remove or reduce:

- staged openers such as "let's dive in" or "the key point is";
- inflated phrases such as "pivotal", "crucial", "landscape", or "testament" when they add no fact;
- decorative bold labels and overformatted lists;
- unsupported "not only X but Y" contrasts;
- generic one-line closers;
- vague authority such as "researchers believe" without a named source;
- chatbot residue and explanation of the drafting process.

Keep:

- technical terms that are necessary;
- calibrated uncertainty;
- citations and exact bibliographic details;
- numbers, metrics, task names, model names, equations, and definitions;
- the user's intended Chinese or English academic voice.

## Research Paper Writing Final Check

After prose polish, check:

- each paragraph has one message;
- the first sentence makes the paragraph role visible;
- transitions show cause, contrast, consequence, or refinement;
- Abstract and Introduction claims are supported by body evidence;
- skeptical-reviewer questions are either answered or listed as open issues.

## Output Contract

Return:

- polished text;
- changed-meaning risk, if any;
- remaining AI-sounding patterns, if any;
- unsupported claims that could not be polished away;
- next gate: citation check, advisor review, experiment result, or final integration.
